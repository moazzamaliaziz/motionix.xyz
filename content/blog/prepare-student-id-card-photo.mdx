Student ID cards are used daily for campus access, library privileges, and discounts. A good photo makes your card look professional and recognizable.

## Standard student ID photo specs

| Specification | Requirement |
|--------------|-------------|
| Size | 2×2 inches or 35×35 mm |
| Resolution | 300 DPI minimum |
| Background | White or light blue |
| Format | JPEG or PNG |
| File size | < 500 KB |
| Recency | Taken within 1 year |

## How to take the photo

1. **White or light blue background.** Plain wall or sheet.
2. **Even lighting.** Natural light from a window, no harsh shadows.
3. **Professional attire.** Business casual or school dress code.
4. **Neutral expression.** No sunglasses, no hats (unless religious).
5. **Head centered.** Face the camera directly.

## What to avoid

- **Casual clothing.** Tank tops, graphic tees, or pajamas.
- **Selfie camera.** The front camera has wider distortion than the rear camera.
- **Busy backgrounds.** Patterned walls, other people, or cluttered rooms.
- **Old photos.** If you look noticeably different, take a new one.

## Processing

1. Take the photo with your phone's rear camera.
2. Upload to a student ID photo tool.
3. Crop to the required dimensions.
4. Download and submit to your school's portal.

Most email providers cap attachments at 20-25 MB. WhatsApp limits videos to 16 MB. Discord caps at 25 MB (free) or 50 MB (Nitro). A 2-minute phone video can easily be 200 MB. Here's how to shrink it.

## Size limits by platform

| Platform | Limit | Recommended approach |
|----------|-------|---------------------|
| Gmail | 25 MB | Compress to 20 MB |
| Outlook | 20 MB | Compress to 15 MB |
| WhatsApp | 16 MB | Compress to 14 MB |
| Discord (free) | 25 MB | Compress to 22 MB |
| Slack | 1 GB | Usually no compression needed |
| iMessage | 100 MB | Compress to 80 MB |

## How video compression works

Video compression removes redundant data:
- **Temporal compression.** Only stores differences between frames, not every full frame.
- **Spatial compression.** Reduces detail in areas the eye can't see (like smooth gradients).
- **Audio compression.** AAC audio at 128 kbps is indistinguishable from the original for most content.

## Step-by-step compression

1. **Check the original file size.** Right-click → Properties (Windows) or Get Info (Mac).
2. **Set a target size.** If the limit is 25 MB, target 20 MB for safety.
3. **Use a browser-based compressor.** No upload, no server, no waiting.
4. **Download the result.** The compressed video is ready to attach.

## Quality settings

| Target | Quality | Use case |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Email (20 MB) | Medium | Good enough for most content |
| WhatsApp (16 MB) | Medium-Low | Acceptable for casual videos |
| Archive (no limit) | High | Keep maximum quality |

## What gets sacrificed

- **Resolution.** 4K → 1080p or 720p for significant savings.
- **Bitrate.** Lower bitrate = smaller file, but more compression artifacts.
- **Audio quality.** 128 kbps AAC is usually fine; 64 kbps is acceptable for voice.
- **Frame rate.** 30 fps → 24 fps saves ~20% with minimal visible difference.

## Common mistakes

- **Compressing already-compressed video.** H.264 → H.264 doesn't help much. You need to reduce resolution or bitrate.
- **Over-compressing.** Below 500 kbps for 1080p, artifacts become very visible.
- **Not checking the result.** Always preview before sending.

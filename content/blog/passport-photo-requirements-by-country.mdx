## Common patterns

- **Most countries** use 35×45 mm (413×531 px at 300 DPI)
- **US and India** use square formats (2×2 inches)
- **White backgrounds** are universally accepted
- **Head size** ranges from 29-36 mm chin-to-crown
- **Glasses** are increasingly banned (US, Canada, Japan)

## Special cases

### Babies and infants
- Eyes don't need to be open in many countries
- No other person should be visible
- Background rules still apply

### Religious head coverings
- Allowed in most countries
- Must not cover the face
- Declaration or letter may be required

### Digital passports
- Some countries accept digital-only applications
- File size limits vary (India: 20-50 KB, US: no limit)
- JPEG format is standard

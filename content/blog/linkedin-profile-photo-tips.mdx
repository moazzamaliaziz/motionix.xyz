LinkedIn profiles with professional photos get 21x more views and 36x more messages than profiles without one. Here's how to make yours count.

## What the data says

- **Smiling** increases connection requests by 14%.
- **Eye contact** (looking at the camera) increases trust ratings by 20%.
- **Professional attire** increases perceived competence by 15%.
- **Simple background** keeps the focus on your face.

## The ideal LinkedIn photo

- **Size:** 400×400 px minimum (1400×1400 recommended)
- **Crop:** Head and shoulders, face fills 60-70% of frame
- **Background:** Simple — office, plain wall, or blurred outdoor
- **Expression:** Friendly smile, eyes open
- **Attire:** One level above the company's dress code

## Common mistakes

- **Cropped group photos.** Other people's shoulders or arms visible.
- **Vacation photos.** Beach, ski slopes, or tourist attractions.
- **Selfies.** Front camera distortion makes your face look wider.
- **Low resolution.** Blurry photos look unprofessional.
- **No photo at all.** Profiles without photos get 90% fewer views.

## How to take a good LinkedIn photo

1. **Stand near a window.** Soft, even lighting across your face.
2. **Use the rear camera.** Less distortion than the selfie camera.
3. **Portrait mode.** Creates a soft background blur.
4. **Crop to square.** LinkedIn displays profile photos as circles.
5. **Wear professional attire.** Solid colors work best.

## Updating your photo

LinkedIn compresses your uploaded photo. To maintain quality:
1. Upload a 1400×1400 px image.
2. LinkedIn will create multiple sizes for different contexts.
3. Check how it looks on desktop and mobile.

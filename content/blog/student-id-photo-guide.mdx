College applications, scholarship portals, and exam registrations all want ID photos — but they can't agree on sizes. Here's a quick reference for the most common student photo requirements.

## Common application photo specs

| Platform | Size | Format | File size | Notes |
|----------|------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Common App | 200×250 px | JPEG/PNG | < 500 KB | Passport-style headshot |
| CSS Profile | 200×250 px | JPEG/PNG | < 500 KB | Same as Common App |
| SAT registration | 600×750 px | JPEG | < 2 MB | Must be recent |
| ACT registration | 600×750 px | JPEG | < 2 MB | Similar to SAT |
| TOEFL | 350×350 px | JPEG | < 100 KB | Square format |
| IELTS | 350×450 px | JPEG | < 100 KB | Portrait format |
| University portals | Varies | JPEG/PNG | Varies | Check each school |

## How to take a student ID photo

1. **Plain background.** White or light gray wall.
2. **Even lighting.** Natural light from a window, no harsh shadows.
3. **Professional attire.** Business casual or whatever the portal specifies.
4. **Neutral expression.** No sunglasses, no hats (unless religious).
5. **Recent photo.** Taken within the last 6 months.

## Processing tips

1. Take the photo with your phone's rear camera (not selfie camera).
2. Upload to a student ID photo tool.
3. Select the platform's preset (Common App, SAT, etc.).
4. Download and upload to the portal.

## Common mistakes

- **Wrong aspect ratio.** A square photo uploaded to a portrait requirement gets cropped badly.
- **File too large.** Some portals reject files over 100 KB.
- **Low resolution.** Blurry photos get flagged by exam proctors.
- **Old photo.** If you look noticeably different, take a new one.

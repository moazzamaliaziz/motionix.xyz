You have a wet-ink signature on paper and need a digital version. Here's how to scan it and create a transparent PNG you can drop into any document.

## Scanning your signature

1. **Sign on white paper.** Use black ink for best contrast.
2. **Sign larger than usual.** A 2-inch signature scans better than a 1-inch one.
3. **Scan at 300 DPI.** Higher resolution = cleaner edges.
4. **Save as PNG or JPEG.** PNG preserves more detail.

## Creating the transparent PNG

1. **Upload the scan** to a signature maker tool.
2. **The tool automatically removes the white paper background.** The AI detects the ink strokes and makes everything else transparent.
3. **Download the transparent PNG.** Ready to drop into any document.

## Manual cleanup (if needed)

If the automatic removal isn't perfect:

1. Open the scan in an image editor.
2. Use the magic wand tool to select the white background.
3. Delete the selection.
4. Clean up any remaining artifacts around the edges.
5. Save as PNG with transparency.

## Best practices

- **Sign with a thick pen.** Fine-point pens create thin strokes that are harder to scan cleanly.
- **Use dark ink.** Black or dark blue ink has the best contrast against white paper.
- **Avoid overlapping strokes.** Clean, separate strokes remove more cleanly.
- **Sign on a flat surface.** Curved paper creates shadows that confuse the background removal.

## Using your digital signature

- **PDF contracts.** Place the PNG in Adobe Acrobat or Preview.
- **Word documents.** Insert → Pictures → Select your signature.
- **Email signatures.** Add to your email client's signature settings.
- **Forms.** Upload to online form builders that accept image signatures.

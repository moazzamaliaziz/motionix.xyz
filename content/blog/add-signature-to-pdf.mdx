You received a PDF contract to sign. The old way: print, sign, scan, email back. The new way: sign digitally in seconds. Here's how.

## Method 1: Create a signature image

1. **Create your signature** using a signature maker tool (draw, type, or upload).
2. **Download as transparent PNG.**
3. **Open the PDF** in a PDF editor (Adobe Acrobat, Preview, or online tool).
4. **Insert the signature image** where needed.
5. **Save and send.**

## Method 2: Use built-in PDF signing

Many apps have built-in signature features:

### Adobe Acrobat
1. Open the PDF → Tools → Fill & Sign
2. Click "Place Signature"
3. Draw, type, or upload your signature
4. Click to place it on the document

### Preview (Mac)
1. Open the PDF → Tools → Annotate → Signature
2. Create a signature using trackpad, camera, or iPhone
3. Click to place it

### Google Drive
1. Open the PDF in Google Drive
2. Use the "Add drawing" feature to add a signature

## Method 3: Online PDF editors

Web-based tools let you upload a PDF, add a signature, and download the signed version. No software installation needed.

## Legal considerations

- **Image signatures** are legally valid in most jurisdictions for everyday agreements.
- **For high-stakes contracts**, use a proper e-signature platform (DocuSign, Adobe Sign) that provides audit trails and identity verification.
- **Check local laws.** Some documents (wills, real estate) may require specific signature types.

## Best practices

- **Use a consistent signature.** Same signature across all documents.
- **Save the signed copy.** Keep a record of what you signed.
- **Verify before sending.** Make sure the signature is placed correctly.

Getting a passport photo rejected is frustrating. You took the time, paid the fee, and waited weeks — only to get a letter saying your photo doesn't meet requirements. Here are the top reasons photos get rejected and how to avoid them.

## Top 5 rejection reasons

### 1. Wrong background color
**Problem:** Off-white, cream, gray, or patterned background.
**Fix:** Use a pure white background. If shooting at home, stand against a white wall or hang a white sheet.

### 2. Incorrect head size or position
**Problem:** Head too small, too large, or off-center.
**Fix:** Use a passport photo tool that auto-positions your head to the correct fraction of the frame.

### 3. Glasses
**Problem:** Glare on lenses, thick frames obscuring eyes.
**Fix:** Remove glasses entirely. The US banned glasses in passport photos in 2016.

### 4. Photo too old
**Problem:** Photo doesn't match current appearance.
**Fix:** Take a new photo within the last 6 months. If you've changed hairstyle, lost/gained weight, or aged noticeably, take a fresh photo.

### 5. Digital manipulation
**Problem:** AI-edited backgrounds, skin smoothing, or other digital alterations.
**Fix:** Use strict-mode tools that only crop and position. Don't use filters or AI editing on passport photos.

## Country-specific gotchas

- **US:** Strictest on digital edits. Any AI manipulation can trigger rejection.
- **India:** File size must be 20-50 KB. Too large or too small = rejection.
- **UK:** Background must be "plain light-coloured" — not necessarily pure white.
- **Schengen:** Photo must be taken within 6 months of application.

## How to avoid rejection

1. **Use a tool with country presets.** Automated sizing eliminates the #1 rejection reason.
2. **Check the preview.** Most tools show you the final result before download.
3. **Print on photo paper.** Matte or glossy — not regular printer paper.
4. **Submit multiple copies.** Include 2-3 identical photos in case one gets damaged.

Product photos with clean backgrounds convert better. Studies show white-background product images increase click-through rates by 25-30% on marketplaces. Here's how to get them without a photo studio.

## The e-commerce background standard

Most marketplaces require:

- **Amazon:** Pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), product fills 85% of frame
- **eBay:** White or light gray, no text or watermarks
- **Etsy:** Any clean background, but white sells best
- **Shopify:** No specific requirement, but white is the convention

## Step-by-step: product photo background removal

1. **Photograph on a contrasting background.** A white product on a white background is the hardest to cut out. Use a dark surface or colored backdrop.
2. **Use even lighting.** Two light sources at 45 degrees eliminate shadows.
3. **Upload to a browser-based tool.** The AI processes locally — your product images stay private.
4. **Check the edges.** Zoom in on complex areas like handles, straps, or transparent packaging.
5. **Download as PNG.** Preserve the transparency for maximum flexibility.

## Batch processing tips

If you have 50+ product photos:

- Shoot them all in the same lighting setup for consistent results
- Use the same background color for all photos
- Process them in one session while the AI model is cached

## Common product photo issues

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Jagged edges | Low-quality input JPEG | Shoot in RAW or high-quality JPEG |
| Shadow included in cutout | Harsh overhead lighting | Use diffused side lighting |
| Transparent parts removed | Model confused by glass/clear plastic | Use a contrasting background behind the item |
| Color fringing | Background color reflecting on product edges | Shoot on neutral gray, not colored backgrounds |

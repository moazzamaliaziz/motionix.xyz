A professional headshot doesn't require a professional photographer. With a modern smartphone and a few minutes, you can take a photo that's good enough for LinkedIn, your resume, and your company's team page.

## What you need

- Smartphone with rear camera (12+ MP)
- Window with natural light
- Plain wall or backdrop
- Someone to take the photo

## Lighting setup

**Best:** Stand facing a large window. Soft, even light across your face.

**Good:** Shoot outdoors in open shade (under a tree, beside a building). Avoid direct sunlight.

**Avoid:** Overhead lighting (creates shadows under eyes), backlighting (silhouette effect), mixed lighting (color casts).

## Camera settings

- **Use the rear camera.** Selfie cameras have wider distortion.
- **Portrait mode on.** Creates a soft background blur (bokeh) that looks professional.
- **No zoom.** Use the main lens at its native focal length.
- **Grid lines on.** Help with composition.

## Posing tips

- **Angle your body 45 degrees.** Turn slightly to the side, then face the camera.
- **Lean slightly forward.** Creates a more engaged, confident posture.
- **Chin slightly down.** Avoids the "looking up at the camera" angle.
- **Relax your shoulders.** Tension shows in photos.

## What to wear

- **Solid colors.** Avoid patterns, stripes, and busy prints.
- **Contrast with background.** Don't wear white against a white wall.
- **Professional but authentic.** Dress like you would for an important meeting.
- **Minimal accessories.** Large earrings and necklaces can be distracting.

## Post-processing

1. Upload the best photo to a headshot tool.
2. Crop to a 1:1 (LinkedIn) or 3:4 (resume) ratio.
3. Optionally swap the background to a solid color.
4. Download and upload.

## LinkedIn-specific tips

- **400×400 px minimum.** LinkedIn displays at 400×400 on desktop.
- **1400×1400 px recommended.** Survives LinkedIn's compression better.
- **Face fills 60-70% of frame.** Head and shoulders only.
- **Professional background.** Office, plain wall, or blurred outdoor setting.

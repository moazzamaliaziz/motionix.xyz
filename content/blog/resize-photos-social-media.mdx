## Tips for social media images

1. **Design for mobile.** 80%+ of social media is consumed on phones.
2. **Leave safe margins.** Platforms crop differently on different devices.
3. **Use 2x resolution.** Export at 2x the display size for Retina screens.
4. **Compress before uploading.** Platforms compress further — start with a clean file.

## Common mistakes

- **Uploading landscape photos for square crops.** Your subject gets cut off.
- **Using low-resolution images.** Blurry photos look unprofessional.
- **Ignoring the circle crop.** Profile photos are displayed as circles — keep important elements centered.

Photoshop's pen tool is still the gold standard for pixel-perfect cutouts. But for 90% of use cases, AI tools produce results that are indistinguishable at normal viewing sizes. Here's when each approach makes sense.

## Speed comparison

| Method | Time per image | Setup time |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Photoshop pen tool | 5-15 minutes | 30 seconds |
| Photoshop Select Subject | 1-3 minutes | 30 seconds |
| AI browser tool | 2-5 seconds | 0 (model downloads once) |

For a batch of 20 product photos, Photoshop takes 1-2 hours. AI takes under a minute.

## Quality comparison

**Where AI wins:**
- Simple backgrounds (white, solid color, gradient)
- Clear subject-background contrast
- Standard product shapes
- Portrait photos with decent lighting

**Where Photoshop wins:**
- Hair and fur at pixel level
- Transparent or translucent objects
- Complex backgrounds with similar colors to the subject
- When you need to manually refine individual pixels

## Cost comparison

| Method | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Photoshop | $20.99/month |
| AI browser tool | Free |
| AI API service | $0.01-0.10 per image |

## The hybrid approach

Professional photographers often use both:

1. **AI for the initial cutout.** Gets you 95% of the way in 2 seconds.
2. **Photoshop for refinement.** Fix the 5% of edges that need manual attention.

This cuts total editing time by 70-80%.

## When to stick with Photoshop

- Product photography for luxury brands where every pixel matters
- Composite images with multiple subjects
- When the background removal is part of a larger Photoshop workflow
- When you need to preserve very fine hair detail for print at 300+ DPI

## When AI is the better choice

- E-commerce product listings
- Social media profile photos
- Resume and passport photos
- Quick mockups and presentations
- Any use case where the image will be viewed at screen resolution

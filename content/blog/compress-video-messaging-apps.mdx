Messaging apps have strict video size limits. A 2-minute phone video can be 200 MB, but WhatsApp caps at 16 MB. Here's how to compress video for each platform.

## Messaging app video limits

| App | Limit | Recommended target |
|-----|-------|-------------------|
| WhatsApp | 16 MB | 14 MB |
| Telegram | 2 GB | No compression needed |
| Discord (free) | 25 MB | 22 MB |
| Discord (Nitro) | 500 MB | No compression needed |
| Slack | 1 GB | No compression needed |
| Signal | 100 MB | 80 MB |
| iMessage | 100 MB | 80 MB |

## How to compress video

1. **Check the original size.** Right-click the file → Properties (Windows) or Get Info (Mac).
2. **Set a target size.** If WhatsApp limit is 16 MB, target 14 MB.
3. **Use a video compressor tool.** Browser-based tools process locally — no upload.
4. **Download and send.** The compressed video is ready.

## What gets sacrificed

- **Resolution.** 4K → 1080p or 720p for significant savings.
- **Bitrate.** Lower bitrate = smaller file, but more artifacts.
- **Audio quality.** 128 kbps AAC is fine for most content.

## Quality settings by platform

| Platform | Resolution | Quality | Notes |
|----------|-----------|---------|-------|
| WhatsApp | 720p | Medium | Most users view on phones |
| Discord | 1080p | Medium-High | Users view on desktop |
| Slack | 1080p | High | Professional context |

## Common mistakes

- **Compressing already-compressed video.** H.264 → H.264 doesn't help much.
- **Over-compressing.** Below 500 kbps for 720p, artifacts become visible.
- **Not checking the result.** Always preview before sending.

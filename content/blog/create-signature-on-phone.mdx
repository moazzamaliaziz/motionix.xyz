Drawing your signature with your finger on a phone screen produces the most natural-looking digital signature. Here's how to do it well.

## Why finger-drawn signatures look best

- **Natural movement.** Your finger moves like a pen, capturing your signature's natural flow.
- **Pressure variation.** Touchscreens detect pressure, creating natural stroke width changes.
- **Speed variation.** Fast strokes and slow strokes create authentic-looking curves.

## Tips for drawing on a phone

1. **Use a large drawing area.** Zoom in if the tool supports it.
2. **Sign at your normal speed.** Don't go slowly — fast strokes look more natural.
3. **Use your index finger.** More control than your thumb.
4. **Rest your wrist on the table.** Stabilizes your hand for smoother strokes.
5. **Practice 2-3 times.** Your first attempt may be shaky.

## What to do with the result

- **Save as transparent PNG.** Works on any document background.
- **Use for PDF contracts.** Insert into Adobe Acrobat or Preview.
- **Add to email signature.** Most email clients support image signatures.
- **Sign online forms.** Upload to form builders that accept images.

## Limitations

- **Small screens are hard.** A phone screen is smaller than a paper signature area.
- **No pen feel.** Your finger slides differently than a pen on paper.
- **Not legally binding in all contexts.** For high-stakes documents, use a proper e-signature platform.
